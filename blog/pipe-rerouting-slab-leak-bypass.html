What Is Overhead Pipe Rerouting and How PEX Lines Bypass Foundation Slabs

Overhead pipe rerouting disconnects leaking sub slab copper lines, running new flexible cross linked polyethylene PEX water lines through attic spaces and interior wall cavities. Rerouting bypasses slab leaks completely, preserving concrete floors and preventing future sub slab pipe failures.

Corroded sub slab copper lines that develop recurring pinholes should not be repeatedly jackhammered. Plumbers licensed under Kentucky State Plumbing Code KRS 318 cap leaking under floor lines at manifold connections, running freeze insulated PEX lines overhead to supply plumbing fixtures safely.
