How Sewer Camera Scoping Detects Sub Slab Drain Line Leaks

Sewer camera inspection inserts a flexible HD self leveling video camera into drain lines through cleanout ports. Built in radio frequency sonde transmitters broadcast exact physical depth and floor coordinates to locate root intrusion, pipe offset, and sub slab cast iron cracking.

Corroded cast iron sewer lines underneath concrete slabs flake and bottom out over decades. plumbing professionals qualified under Kentucky State Plumbing Code KRS 318 perform video camera diagnostics to confirm whether trenchless CIPP epoxy relining or spot repair offers the best solution.
