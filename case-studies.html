Case Study 1: Residential Slab Leak

Under-Slab Copper Pipe Leak on Fairview Drive

The Problem: An Owensboro homeowner on Fairview Drive contacted us after discovering a localized warm spot on their kitchen linoleum floor and receiving a water bill showing a 300% consumption increase. They suspected an under-floor leak but dreaded having their entire kitchen slab excavated.

Diagnostics: We deployed high-frequency acoustic listening equipment (Fisher XLT-30) to trace the sound of pressurized water under the concrete. By testing localized sound frequency signatures and using digital moisture meters, we pinpointed the exact pinhole leak location beneath a 4-inch concrete slab directly in front of the kitchen sink.

Resolution: Instead of destroying the entire kitchen floor, we performed a targeted excavation of only 1 square foot of concrete. We cut out the corroded section of the bare copper pipe, which had failed due to soil electrolysis, and soldered in a highly durable section of Type K copper pipe. The soil was compacted, and the concrete slab was repoured, restoring full plumbing pressure with zero foundation damage.

Case Study 2: Trenchless Utility Repair

Driveway Main Water Line Pull in Utica, KY

The Problem: A property owner in Utica, KY experienced a severe water main line rupture. The leak was located directly beneath their newly paved asphalt driveway. A traditional excavation would require cutting a 60-foot trench through the asphalt, causing thousands of dollars in property damage.

Diagnostics: Using ground microphone systems and acoustic line tracing, we mapped the trajectory of the water line and verified the location of the break without pavement disruption.

Resolution: We opted for a trenchless pipe pulling method using High-Density Polyethylene (HDPE) piping. By digging only two small access pits - one near the water meter and one near the house foundation - we pulled a brand-new, seamless 1-inch HDPE water line directly through the old pipe path, completely bypassing the rupture and preserving the driveway.

Case Study 3: Commercial Sewer Inspection

Sewer Camera Diagnostics & Jetting in Philpot, KY

The Problem: A commercial storefront on Hwy 54 in Philpot was experiencing recurring sewer backups, causing business interruptions. Plumbers had previously snaked the line, but the blockage returned within weeks.

Diagnostics: We fed a RIDGID SeeSnake self-leveling digital sewer camera down the line. At 42 feet out, we identified a massive root intrusion from a nearby tree that had offset a clay pipe joint, catching debris and causing complete blockages.

Resolution: We utilized high-pressure hydro-jetting to clear out all tree roots and debris, restoring flow. We then excavated a small localized area directly above the offset joint and replaced the broken clay pipe section with Schedule 40 PVC. The repair was completed and tested within a single afternoon, preventing future backups.

Attic Bypass Masonville, KY
Case Study 4: Advanced Repiping

Under-Slab Attic Bypass in Masonville, KY

The Problem: A home in Masonville, KY suffered from multiple recurring slab leaks in a hot water line beneath the master bathroom floor. Breaking through the tiled walk-in shower concrete floor would require an expensive bathroom remodel.

Diagnostics: FLIR E6-XT thermal imaging cameras revealed hot water pooling beneath the concrete, confirming multiple pinholes in the copper line. Due to the high risk of future leaks in other parts of the same line, localized concrete breakouts were ruled out.

Resolution: We engineered and executed a complete attic bypass. We cut and isolated the compromised sub-slab copper hot water line at both ends. We then ran a brand-new, flexible Uponor PEX hot water line through the attic space inside thick insulation sleeves to prevent freezing. This successfully restored hot water to the bathroom with zero damage to the tile floor.
