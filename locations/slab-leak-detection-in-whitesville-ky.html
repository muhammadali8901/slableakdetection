Locally Relevant Frequently Asked Questions

Q How does hard water cause slab leaks in Whitesville KY?

Whitesville and surrounding areas often experience high mineral content in the local water supply. Over time, scale buildup and chemical pitting erode copper pipes under concrete slabs, leading to pinhole slab leaks.

Q What should I do if I find a warm spot on my Whitesville floor?

A warm spot on concrete, tile, or linoleum floors is a classic indicator of a hot water line slab leak. Turn off your water main immediately and call our 24/7 emergency dispatch to isolate the leak.
