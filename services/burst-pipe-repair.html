Burst Pipe Repair Services in Owensboro

A burst pipe is a catastrophic emergency that can cause thousands of dollars in water damage in minutes. We provide 24/7 rapid response burst pipe repairs for frozen lines, water main ruptures, and pressurized pipe failures, cutting out damaged lines and restoring water safely.

Signs and Causes of Burst Pipe Repair Services Issues

Common Signs & Symptoms:

  • Gushing or spraying water coming from ceilings, walls, or crawls.
  • Water pooling under baseboards or along concrete floors.
  • A sudden, complete loss of water pressure inside the house.
  • Rumbling or hissing noises inside wall cavities when taps are off.

Root Causes:

  • Freezing winter temperatures expanding water inside uninsulated pipes.
  • High household water pressure (exceeding 80 PSI) weakening joint seams.
  • Severe metal pipe corrosion causing thin walls to rupture.

Service Frequently Asked Questions

Q What should I do first if a water pipe bursts?

Go to your main water shut-off valve immediately and turn it clockwise to shut off all incoming water. Open faucets to drain remaining pressure, and call us for emergency dispatch.

Q How do I prevent my water lines from freezing in winter?

Insulate pipes in unheated areas (crawlspaces, attics), keep cabinets open to let heat circulate, and let faucets drip slowly during extreme freezes below 20 degrees.
