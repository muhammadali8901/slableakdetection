What Is Electronic Leak Detection and How Microphones Isolate Sub Slab Leaks

Electronic leak detection relies on acoustic amplification equipment and radio frequency pipe locators to find pressurized water leaks under concrete foundations. Ground microphones filter out ambient home noise to isolate escaping water vibrations through six inches of slab without floor removal.

When underground copper or PEX water supply lines crack, pressurized water creates sound frequencies between 200 Hz and 800 Hz. our qualified plumbing professionals under Kentucky State Plumbing Code KRS 318 use digital ground sensors that convert audio vibrations into graphic visual spectrums, enabling exact leak site marking.
