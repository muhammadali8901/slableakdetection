Tools and Methods for Leak Detection

Tools & Equipment

Thermal Imaging Camera

Used to detect temperature changes in walls and floors, indicating potential leaks.

Moisture Meter

Measures moisture levels in various materials to identify areas affected by leaks.

Acoustic Listening Devices

Allows technicians to listen for the sound of water escaping from pipes, pinpointing leak locations.

Video Inspection Equipment

Pressure Testing
