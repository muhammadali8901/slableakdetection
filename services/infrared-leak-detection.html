What Is Infrared Leak Detection and How Thermal Cameras Spot Hot Water Leaks

Infrared leak detection uses thermal thermography cameras to capture surface temperature differences caused by leaking underground water lines. Thermal imagers detect hot water migrating through concrete slab foundations, pinpointing sub slab leaks visually without breaking tile, laminate, or hardwood floors.

Hot water lines running under concrete slabs transfer heat upward when copper fittings crack. Our licensed master plumbers under Kentucky State Plumbing Code KRS 318 use thermographic cameras calibrated to tenth of a degree temperature variations. This non invasive scan highlights hot water plumes under flooring before moisture causes mold or subfloor warpage.
