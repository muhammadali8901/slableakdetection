What Is Sub Slab Pipe Rerouting and How Overhead PEX Lines Bypass Concrete

Sub slab pipe rerouting disconnects corroded under floor water pipes, running new flexible cross linked polyethylene PEX lines through attic spaces and wall cavities. Overhead rerouting completely eliminates future foundation slab leaks without breaking ceramic tile or hardwood floors.

When sub slab copper piping develops recurring pinholes, jackhammering multiple spots weakens foundation integrity. Plumbers licensed under Kentucky State Plumbing Code KRS 318 cap leaking sub floor lines at manifold connections, running freeze resistant PEX lines overhead to supply sinks, showers, and toilets safely.
