What Is Sewer Line Leak Detection and How Video Camera Inspections Work

Sewer line leak detection pinpoints breaks, root penetrations, and joint separations in drain waste lines under concrete slabs. Plumbers push self leveling high definition cameras into sewer cleanouts, pairing video feeds with radio transmitters to locate leaks above ground without floor demolition.

Sub slab sewer line leaks cause severe structural foundation damage. Escaping wastewater washes away soil under concrete, creating air voids that lead to floor sagging. Our licensed Kentucky plumbers follow Kentucky State Plumbing Code KRS 318 rules, performing hydrostatic pressure tests with inflatable test balls to verify whether drain lines hold water pressure.

5 Warning Signs of a Sewer Line Leak Under Your Foundation Slab

Common signs of a sub slab sewer leak include foul sewer gas smells near drains, slow lavatory drainage, indoor fruit fly or drain fly infestations, floor tile cracking, and soil depressions near foundation perimeters.

Sewer Gas Odors Indoors

Foul hydrogen sulfide odors rising from drain openings or floor seams signal cracked underground waste lines.

Frequent Drain Backups

Toilets gurgling or bathtubs draining slowly across multiple fixtures points to sub slab main sewer line blockage.
