What Is Water Line Leak Detection and How Plumbers Find Hidden Supply Line Leaks

Water line leak detection isolates hidden main supply pipe breaks between municipal water meters and home foundation walls. Plumbers use acoustic ground microphones and static pressure manifold checks to pinpoint underground leaks without destroying lawns or landscaping.

Underground water lines laid under soil endure ground shifting and pipe corrosion over time. Our plumbing professionals operating under Kentucky State Plumbing Code KRS 318 perform static pressure isolation checks to verify main line leakage before marking exact underground repair locations.
