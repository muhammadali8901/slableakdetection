Tools and Methods for Leak Detection

Tools & Equipment

Thermal Imaging Camera

Used to detect temperature variations in walls and floors, helping identify hidden leaks.

Acoustic Listening Devices

These devices amplify the sound of leaks, allowing technicians to pinpoint their location accurately.

Moisture Meters

Used to measure moisture levels in walls and floors, indicating potential leak areas.

Video Inspection Equipment

Allows for a visual inspection of pipes, identifying blockages or leaks without invasive measures.

Methods & Standards

Acoustic Leak Detection

This method involves listening for the sound of water escaping from pipes, which helps locate leaks in hard-to-reach areas.

Infrared Thermography

This non-invasive method uses infrared cameras to detect temperature differences, indicating potential leaks.

Moisture Mapping

Moisture mapping helps visualize areas of water intrusion by measuring moisture levels across surfaces.
