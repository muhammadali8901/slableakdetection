Water Line Repair Services in Owensboro

Your home's main water line supplies all incoming fresh water. When it cracks or breaks under your lawn, you risk low household water pressure, yard sinkholes, and high utility bills. We specialize in minimally invasive repairs, using trenchless technologies to fix lines without destroying driveways or landscaping.

Signs and Causes of Water Line Repair Services Issues

Common Signs & Symptoms:

  • Persistent wet or soggy spots on your lawn when it hasn't rained.
  • A sudden, severe drop in water pressure across all fixtures.
  • Water meter spins constantly even when all indoor water is shut off.
  • Discolored, rusty, or silt-heavy water coming from your faucets.

Root Causes:

  • Natural ground settling and thermal contraction causing joints to shear.
  • Freeze-thaw cycles expanding water and cracking older materials.
  • Aggressive tree root systems burrowing into pipe joints for moisture.
  • Electrolysis or chemical soil corrosion attacking older copper lines.

Our Water Line Repair Services Repair & Diagnostic Process

01

Service Frequently Asked Questions

Q Who is responsible for the main water line repair?

Homeowners are responsible for the main water line running from the utility meter pit (usually near the sidewalk or road curb) up to the building's interior. The city water department is responsible for the line before the meter.

Q What is trenchless water line replacement?

Trenchless replacement (pipe pulling) involves running a heavy steel cable through the damaged main line to pull a new, highly durable HDPE pipe into place while splitting the old pipe apart, requiring only two small access holes.
